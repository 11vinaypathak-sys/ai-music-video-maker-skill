# Direction and storyboard worksheet

## Creative intention

- What should the viewer feel or understand?
- Who is it for, and where will they encounter it?
- What is the central subject or idea?
- Which references clarify the direction, and what is useful about each?
- What has already been approved and should remain?

## Focused revision

Record the observed issue, intended change, material to preserve and result to review. Resolve one meaningful creative uncertainty with the next test rather than regenerating everything.

## Final review

Watch and listen for continuity, pacing, framing, readable text and sound alignment. Check complete playback and the agreed export format. Confirm that the result answers the creative intention.
