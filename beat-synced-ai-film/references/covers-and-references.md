# Reference-led covers and typography

## Research before interpretation

When asked for Pinterest references, inspect actual pins if available. If search cannot access Pinterest, try an available authorized browser and disclose the access limitation rather than presenting unrelated results as Pinterest research. Share direct reference links and show usable previews. Do not infer authorship from the name of the person who saved a pin.

For each selected reference, write a short visual contract: silhouette, image scale, type placement, palette, texture, optical/material mechanism and what will change for this project. Recommend a specific combination only after showing the references when the user requests research first.

## Effects with similar names can be very different

| User language | Possible construction | What to confirm visually |
|---|---|---|
| Prism | Smooth refractive solid distorting an upper-face region | Is the surface warped, transparent or opaque? |
| Cutout | Displaced photographic plane or torn printed layer | Shape, edge thickness, gap and shadow |
| Bulging out | Continuous surface swelling toward the camera | Smooth curvature versus rigid extruded blocks |
| Motion layers | Closely offset exposures following one gesture | Direction, separation, grain and which face stays sharp |
| Energy | Light emitted from a specific point | Propagation, occlusion and illumination of nearby surfaces |

A failed example from development: interpreting "cutout prism" as a thick stack of rigid face slabs when the reference implied smooth optical deformation. Return to the reference and resolve the mechanism before another generation. More detail or more colour cannot fix the wrong construction.

## Preserve identity and composition

Use the actual approved portrait as the identity reference. An attractive generated derivative is not a replacement for it. Label identity, scene and style references separately. Keep a readable facial anchor and use colour selectively. A monochrome portrait with one precise coloured intervention may communicate more clearly at thumbnail size than the whole climax in one poster.

Make the vertical cover a designed composition; do not automatically crop a wide film and lose its main action. Check the current platform's cover/grid behaviour before relying on exact safe-zone numbers. Keep essential face and title central enough to review across likely crops. Cover creation alone does not authorize publishing, changing Stories or account metadata.

## Typography

Show actual words in the actual frame. Generated font-style previews are approximate; apply available licensed native fonts for final editable text and verify the rendered result. Name substitutions instead of claiming an exact font match. Check requested word spacing separately from letter tracking. Use restrained colour on type only if it retains contrast and does not fight the image.

## Image prompt scaffold

```text
Identity reference: [approved portrait; features that must remain exact].
Edit target: [current artwork; what stays unchanged].
Style reference: [actual viewed example; optical/material mechanism].
Composition: [aspect, face size/position, negative space, title region].
Intervention: [precise region], constructed as [material/geometry], with
[depth, surface continuity, shadows, optical distortion, movement direction].
Texture and palette: [specific roles, contrast, grain scale].
Exact text: [verbatim words, line breaks, typographic hierarchy].
Avoid: [the few observed failures relevant to this iteration].
```

After generation, inspect identity, mechanism, text, image dimensions and crop. Save the useful result and prompt. Keep unapproved alternatives out of the final delivery package.
