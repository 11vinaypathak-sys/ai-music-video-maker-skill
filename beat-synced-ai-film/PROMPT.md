# Portable prompt: beat-synced AI film

Copy the text below into an AI assistant and supply your own project files, references and current request. This prompt does not grant the assistant access to tools or accounts it does not already have.

---

Help me create or refine a coherent short AI music film using the assets and references I provide. Start by checking the current approved project and asking only for essential missing information. Preserve my chosen subject identity, scene, good edits and audio. Work in this assistant with its available tools.

Treat reference research, image storyboarding, video generation, editing and delivery as separate modes. If I ask to see references first, show actual linked references before generating. Translate their visual mechanisms precisely: optical refraction, displaced photographic layers, rigid extrusion and additive light are different effects.

Map musical timing from accessible audio and listening; do not invent beat timestamps from a song title. Design cuts and accents around the musical structure. A cut is not required on every beat. Define each shot's start pose, screen-space direction, camera path, effects propagation and final handoff. Keep body, camera, effects and edit motion independently specified but causally connected. Calm bodies can coexist with very fast effects.

For impacts, specify the force origin, torso acceleration, delayed limbs, clothing inertia and recovery. Let a pullback reveal scale; keep energy layers evolving during a brief frozen-body moment. For speed ramps, preserve a readable approach and anchor before accelerating out. Check optical-flow distortions and adjacent-shot continuity.

Use my approved source face as the identity anchor. For continuation, prefer the actual preceding shot's final frame. Before paid generation, verify provider, model, duration, resolution, audio setting and live price against my authorization. Do not silently change providers, retry unknown-status jobs, exceed the total cap or assume another take is authorized. Show raw takes first when requested. Track quoted versus confirmed spending separately for each provider's credit units.

Refine the existing editable project in small targeted passes. Track lights and overlays to geometry, match occlusion and light spill, vary effect depth and timing, and use short motivated glitches instead of frame tearing or frozen chunks. Check crop edges under camera shake. Keep one clear WIP and preserve source assets.

For a cover, use my own imagery in a deliberate composition with legible typography. Show the visual direction before assuming approval. A generated type preview is not an exact editable font. Do not publish or modify my social account without authorization.

For a wide film on a vertical canvas, preserve the active picture and approved audio. Treat surrounding surface, movie and native text as separate layers. If I want a physical engraved-screen illusion, use consistent highlights, recessed shadows and smooth black fades; do not assume that depth requires animation. Approve one still before applying it to the full film. Isolate generated decorative backgrounds from any mockup movie image or lettering.

Before delivery, check the full video decode, dimensions, frame count, duration, black intervals, audio and representative frames. Verify source dependencies and ZIP integrity if packaging. State exactly what was verified, what remains uncertain and whether anything was actually published. Deliver usable artifacts, not only prompts.

My current request and constraints:
[Describe the film or specific revision; attach assets; state any budget and output requirements.]
